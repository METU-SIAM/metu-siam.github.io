The Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics is the international
professional society for applied mathematics and computational science.
Student chapters exist so that interest in those subjects can live on a
campus — talks, workshops, and a way to meet faculty and students from
other departments.

METU SIAM was founded in **2004** at the [Institute of Applied Mathematics]({{ site.data.site.links.iam_en }}).
It is the first SIAM student chapter in Turkey. A volunteer group of graduate
students has run it since then, along the lines of SIAM’s own aims: to connect
chapter members with faculty, to share how applied mathematics is used in
industry, and to pass on career information.

Activities include seminars, workshops, field trips, social events,
competitions, and participation in SIAM meetings. The scientific home of the
chapter is IAM’s four programmes — Actuarial Sciences, Cryptography, Financial
Mathematics, and Scientific Computing — but membership is not limited to IAM.

## Aims of the chapter

- Introduce SIAM and its activities at METU and in Turkey.
- Remain international, intercultural, and interdisciplinary.
- Increase public awareness of applied mathematics and related fields.
- Broaden students’ perspectives in those fields.
- Improve the conversation between academic applied mathematics / computational
  science and practitioners in industry.

These sit under SIAM’s own objects: furthering the application of mathematics
to industry and science; promoting research that yields methods useful there;
and providing a medium for exchanging ideas between mathematicians and other
technical people.
